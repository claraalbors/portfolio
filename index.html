Info

Clara Albors is a colorist and VFX compositor. She works in colour grading and compositing for feature documentaries, short films, series, music videos and advertising.

Her work spans the full image pipeline: from on-set DIT to final delivery in broadcast and digital formats. She works in DaVinci Resolve, NukeX and Silverstack, managing deliverables independently across simultaneous projects with tight deadlines.

Recent credits include Retrato de una cerilla (Docs Valencia 2026), Coquito con Mortadela (Docs Valencia 2025, Miami Film Festival 2026), Cecilia, tan lluny, tan aprop (Docs Valencia 2025, Mirades Directed By Women 2025), and Only Snuff (2024 — Mórbido Film Fest, Milwaukee Horror FF, Cabin in the Woods FF, among others).
