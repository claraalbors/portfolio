The Project

Un perro ladra detrás del depósito is an experimental documentary that portrays the Valencian huerta as a territory on the verge of extinction. A project with bold aesthetic decisions where the color had to push the material to the limit: aggressive and stylized, capable of showing the decay, the harshness of the sun and the density of the Valencian night without losing a high standard of quality in every shot.
