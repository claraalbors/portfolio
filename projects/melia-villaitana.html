The Project

Meliá Villaitana is a commercial for Meliá Hotels International shot under multiple conditions: interiors and exteriors across the resort, night scenes, underwater footage and drone shots, with material from a BMPCC 6K, Sony A7 III and an underwater camera. The work consisted of unifying very different image sources under a look consistent with the brand's visual identity, with a polished finish that met the standards of an international corporate client. The process included compositing and clean-up to resolve imperfections in the footage and guarantee the final quality of every shot.
