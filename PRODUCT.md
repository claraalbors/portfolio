# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Film directors and producers actively looking to hire a colorist or VFX compositor for their next project. They arrive with intent — a credit check, a work sample scan, a gut feel for whether Clara's aesthetic matches their vision. Time-pressed, visual-fluent, they decide fast.

## Product Purpose

Clara Albors' professional portfolio. Its job is to communicate competence, taste, and range in a single scroll — and make it frictionless to reach out. Success = a director trusting their project to her after 90 seconds on the page.

## Brand Personality

Minimal · Technical · Expert. The work earns attention; the site doesn't demand it. Quiet authority, no self-promotion noise.

## References

- rinayang.com — sparse, image-led, the work fills the frame
- BBH (bartleboglehegarty.com) — confident editorial restraint, typographic hierarchy
- DNEG (dneg.com) — industry-grade credibility, dark, cinematic, tools-forward

## Anti-references

- Generic freelancer / agency templates (Squarespace-default, card grids, skills-bars)
- Overly minimal / museum-cold (so restrained it has no voice)
- Instagram aesthetic (soft gradients, pastel palette, caption-first)

## Design Principles

1. **The work is the hero.** Every layout decision defers to the stills and footage. Nothing competes.
2. **Credibility through specificity.** Festival credits, tool names, client names — concrete detail does more than adjectives.
3. **Quiet, not empty.** Restraint with intent. White (or dark) space should feel deliberate, not absent.
4. **Expert confidence.** No hedging. No "aspiring." The site speaks as a working professional to peers.
5. **Friction-free contact.** One click from any scroll position to start a conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best effort. Readable contrast, keyboard-navigable, no hard WCAG compliance target.
